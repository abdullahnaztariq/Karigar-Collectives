Preface

Executive Summary

Karigar Collective is a premium handmade craft marketplace built on a product-first philosophy. Everything flows from the product — how it is priced, where it is sold, and how it is marketed. This 4P plan is the strategic foundation for a lean, culturally-rich startup entering an underserved market.

The global handmade and artisan goods market is projected to exceed $1.2 trillion by 2027. Pakistan — home to some of the world's oldest living craft traditions — has virtually no premium digital representation in this market. Karigar Collective is built to close that gap by treating handmade Pakistani craft not as a cottage product, but as a cultural asset deserving premium positioning, thoughtful pricing, strategic distribution, and storytelling-led promotion.

Product Strategy

What we sell & how we differentiate it
Karigar Collective's product is not simply a handmade object — it is a curated cultural artefact with documented provenance. The product exists in three layers simultaneously.

The Three-Layer Product Model

Layer 1 · The Physical Object
The tangible handmade item — ceramic, textile, woodwork, leather. Every product must pass a 4-point curation test: demonstrably handmade, made by a verified Pakistani artisan, meets minimum quality threshold, and photographed to Karigar visual standards. Without all four, it does not list.
Layer 2 · The Story Product
The Artisan Passport attached to every item — the maker's portrait, city, craft tradition, generational legacy, and a 60-second workshop video via QR code. The story is the product. Without it, a Karigar ceramic is just a ceramic. With it, it becomes a cultural object with a human behind it.
Layer 3 · The Experience
The full ownership ritual — branded packaging, handwritten artisan note, care instruction card, and post-purchase email story sequence. The experience of owning a Karigar product must be as beautiful as the object itself. This layer turns buyers into ambassadors.

Product USP — 5 Differentiators

Artisan Passport
Every product has a verified maker profile — photograph, video, craft history. No competitor can buy this. It is built through relationships, trust, and field visits. This is our deepest moat.
Curatorial Gatekeeping
A jury-based approval process. We don't list everything — we curate. Scarcity and selectivity communicate premium before the customer reads a single word of copy.
The Imperfection Premium
We celebrate irregularities that prove handmade — uneven glaze, visible brush strokes, asymmetric weave. Positioned not as flaws but as authenticity marks. The imperfection is the point.
Regional Heritage Coding
Every product tagged with region, craft tradition, and approximate age of technique. Customers aren't buying décor — they're buying a documented piece of Pakistani heritage.
Limited Edition Drops
Monthly "Edit" drops of curated thematic collections. Once a batch sells, it may not return for weeks. This creates genuine scarcity — the opposite of the infinite-scroll Amazon experience.

Pricing Strategy

How we price, position & protect our value
Price is not just a number — it is the loudest signal of what the product is worth. Our pricing strategy communicates premium quality, respects artisan labour, and positions us firmly above the commodity market.

Pricing Philosophy — Value-Based, Not Cost-Plus

We price based on the cultural and aesthetic value our product delivers — not simply what it costs to produce. This is critical for three reasons:

  • It protects artisan margins — makers receive a fair base regardless of how we mark up.
  • It signals premium quality — underpriced handmade craft triggers doubt, not desire, in our target buyer's mind.
  • It creates meaningful room for promotions — a 20% Eid discount on a PKR 5,000 item feels generous; the same on a PKR 500 item is irrelevant.

Discount & Promotion Rules

The Golden Rule: We never discount more than 20%, and we never discount without a story. Every discount needs a reason — Eid, a new drop launch, a loyalty reward, or a retiring artisan's last batch. Discounts without narrative cheapen the brand permanently.
  • Welcome Discount (10%): One-time, given in welcome email Day 7. Expires in 30 days. Drives first purchase conversion.
  • Seasonal Promotions (15–20%): Tied to Eid (twice yearly), Independence Day, year-end. Always with a campaign narrative.
  • Loyalty Discount (10% ongoing): Unlocked after second purchase. Applied automatically — positioned as a reward, not desperation.
  • Referral Credit (PKR 500): Earned when a friend makes their first purchase. Feels like a gift, not a coupon.
  • No Flash Sales, No Daily Deals: Brand killers in the premium segment. Never.

Pricing Psychology Tactics

  • Price Anchoring: Show the most expensive item in a category first. The PKR 14,000 rug makes the PKR 4,500 cushion cover feel reasonable.
  • Artisan Earnings Line: On product pages: "Artisan earns PKR X from this purchase." Customers who see this convert at higher rates — it removes guilt, adds pride.
  • Clean Pricing — No .99: PKR 3,500 not PKR 3,499. Charm pricing signals mass market. Our pricing is confident and precise.
  • True Scarcity Language: "Only 3 pieces left in this batch" or "This artisan completes 4–5 pieces per month." Real scarcity, never manufactured.

Place & Distribution

Where & how customers buy from us
Place is every point where a customer encounters, explores, and purchases from Karigar Collective. Our strategy is digital-first with deliberate physical presence — owning the premium shelf wherever our buyer shops.

Primary Channel — karigar.pk (D2C Website)

Our owned eCommerce website is the core of everything. All channels drive traffic here. This gives us full control over the brand experience, customer data, and margins — none of which exist when selling on third-party marketplaces.

  • Platform: Shopify — scalable, with excellent Pakistani payment gateway integrations (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank transfer, COD).
  • UX Principle: Product pages structured as editorial features, not catalogue listings. Artisan name and portrait appear above the fold. The story appears before the price.
  • Payment Mix: JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank transfer, COD (domestic) + Stripe/PayPal (international). Incentivise digital payment with a PKR 100 "digital rebate" to reduce COD dependency over time.
  • Mobile-First: 75%+ of Pakistani eCommerce is mobile. The site must perform flawlessly on a mid-range Android phone.

Secondary Digital Channels

Instagram Shop
Product catalogue synced with website. Every post and Reel can be tagged as shoppable. This shortens the path from discovery to purchase — critical for impulse buys from new audiences who would otherwise lose momentum navigating to the website.
WhatsApp Business
Dedicated number for queries, order tracking, and corporate gifting inquiries. In Pakistan, many buyers complete a purchase more comfortably via WhatsApp than a website checkout. Proper response protocol and product catalogue essential.
Daraz (Selective)
List 15–20 entry-tier products only as a discovery channel. Price 10% higher than website to protect brand positioning and incentivise buying direct. Never discount on Daraz. It is a discovery funnel, not a home.

Physical Distribution Strategy

Month 3+

Place 10–15 Karigar products in 3–5 design-forward cafés and lifestyle boutiques in Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad. Products displayed with QR codes linking to the artisan's profile. Sold on consignment (70/30 split). The café becomes a living showroom at zero rent cost.

Setup Kit: Each partner receives a branded shelf unit, product cards with QR codes, a framed "Karigar x [Café Name]" collab sign, and a monthly restocking schedule.

🏪
Quarterly Pop-Up Markets
Every Quarter

Presence at curated lifestyle markets — Lahore Numaish, Karachi artisan markets, Islamabad craft fairs. The physical experience creates brand memories that digital cannot: the smell of natural dyes, the weight of a hand-thrown ceramic, the texture of woven fabric. Buyers who purchase at a pop-up have a 68% higher repeat purchase rate online.

Booth Design: Warm lighting, wooden display fixtures, artisan portrait prints, workshop video playing on tablet. A Karigar brand installation — not a market stall. Every pop-up becomes 2 weeks of social content.

✈️
International — Diaspora Distribution
Month 7+

UAE Pilot: 50-order test to UAE. Partner with a Pakistani-owned boutique in Dubai for local fulfilment (eliminating customs complexity). Target communities in Jumeirah, Bur Dubai, Sharjah.

UK Pilot: Consignment placement in a Pakistani diaspora lifestyle store in Bradford, Birmingham, or London. Online shipping via Royal Mail tracked service.

Shipping Standard: International orders in premium rigid box, not bubble wrap envelope. Branded outer packaging. Tracked shipping. Customs documentation handled by Karigar — the customer never worries about paperwork.

Promotion Strategy

How we create awareness, desire & conversion
Promotion for Karigar is not advertising — it is cultural storytelling at scale. We do not promote products; we publish culture. Every piece of communication should make someone feel something before they feel an urge to buy.

Target Audience Segments

The Aesthetic Gen-Z
Ages 18–26. Discovers on TikTok, saves to Pinterest, buys on Instagram. Motivated by visual uniqueness and the "hidden gem" discovery. Trigger: Process content and beautiful object photography.
The Nostalgic Millennial
Ages 27–42. Reconnects with heritage through objects. Higher disposable income and fierce loyalty once earned. Trigger: Artisan narratives, cultural storytelling, gifting occasions.
The Diaspora Buyer
Pakistanis abroad — UAE, UK, North America. High AOV, emotionally driven. Buying Karigar is an act of cultural identity. Trigger: "Piece of home" messaging and heritage imagery.
The Gift Shopper
Seasonal buyer driving outsized Eid and wedding revenue. Needs gift-ready options with premium packaging. Trigger: Curated gift sets, beautiful unboxing content, gifting guides.

Social Media — Platform-Specific Playbooks

Instagram · @karigar.collective
Brand Home

Instagram is our brand magazine — every post should feel like a page from a premium craft publication. Documentary-intimate, not corporate-polished.

5 Content Pillars

  • Maker Chronicles (35%): Artisan Reels at work. Real workshops, real faces. Raw ambient sound over music. Documentary intimacy beats production quality here.
  • Object Study (25%): Extreme close-up product photography. The grain of wood, the irregularity of glaze, the imperfection that proves handmade. Make craft look as premium as luxury jewellery.
  • Styled World (20%): Karigar products in real homes, natural light, lived-in settings. Not showroom staging — the product fits into a life.
  • Cultural Context (15%): Educational carousels about specific craft traditions. Earns saves, builds authority, drives follows.
  • Community Mirror (5%): Reshared customer photos and UGC. Validates the brand through real buyer experience.

Reel Concepts That Perform

  • "30 seconds with Ustad Bashir" — full craft process in 30 seconds, no narration, ambient sound only. These consistently hit 100K+ views in the maker category.
  • "The imperfection is the point" — close-up of handmade "flaws" with text: "This is not a defect. This is proof it was made by a human."
  • "Thrift store vs. Karigar" — room transformation replacing generic décor with handmade pieces. Hook: "This is what your home looks like when it has a story."
  • "Unboxing as ritual" — slow ASMR-style unboxing. Cotton bag, tissue, artisan note, product reveal. Pair with ambient trending audio.

Paid Advertising Strategy (Month 4+)

The Rule: We do not run paid media until organic posts demonstrate at least 3–4% engagement rate. We boost what works — never what we hope will work.

Meta Campaign Structure

  • Awareness (PKR 15K/month): Artisan process Reels to cold audiences interested in home décor, sustainable living, and Pakistani heritage.
  • Traffic (PKR 10K/month): Carousel ads to product pages and blog posts. Objective: qualified visitors.
  • Conversion (PKR 20K/month): Retargeting website visitors, Instagram engagers (30-day window), email lookalike audiences. Objective: Purchase.

Creative Principles

  • First 2 seconds must arrest attention — artisan hands in motion, a product reveal, a before/after.
  • Lead with story, not product: "Zainab has been weaving for 30 years." Then the CTA.
  • Test 3 creative variants per ad set: video, carousel, single image.
  • UGC customer photos convert 3× better than brand photography in retargeting ads.

Email Marketing Strategy

Email is the one channel we own completely — no algorithm, no platform risk, no pay-to-play. An engaged list of 5,000 is worth more than 50,000 social followers who only hear from us when Meta allows it.

Lead Magnet — "The Karigar Field Guide"

A free 20-page PDF: "12 Pakistani Crafts Worth Owning." Visual, educational, genuinely valuable before any purchase. Pop-up triggered at 40 seconds on site. Expected conversion rate: 35–50%.

Welcome Sequence (5 emails over 10 days)

  1. Day 0: Deliver guide + brand philosophy. Subject: "Your Field Guide is inside — and so is a story you won't forget."
  2. Day 2: One artisan profile, full story, their collection. Soft sell. Subject: "This is Ustad Ahmed. These are his hands."
  3. Day 4: Founder story — the mission behind Karigar. Subject: "The honest reason we started this."
  4. Day 7: First product email. Curated carousel. 10% welcome discount valid 30 days. Subject: "These 8 pieces are currently in our workshop."
  5. Day 10: Quality and ethics explainer — how we vet artisans, ensure fair wages. Subject: "Every artisan on our platform earns above market rate. Here's how."
